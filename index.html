Curriculum

Ten sessions, in the order a real acquisition happens

Each session is a live, 90-minute class with instruction, worked examples, and Q&A. Sessions are numbered rather than dated so the same syllabus runs every cohort; your calendar of dates is sent at enrollment. Recordings are posted within 24 hours.

1

Orientation and the investor's decision framework

How multifamily ownership works, the roles in a deal, and how to define your own criteria before you look at a single property.

You leave with: a written buy box (used for the refund-window assignment).

2

Market selection

Reading population, employment, and supply data for a metro and submarket. How to compare two markets on the same scale.

You leave with: a completed market scorecard for one metro.

3

Underwriting fundamentals

Income, expenses, net operating income, debt service, and the assumptions that matter most. The XSITE underwriting spreadsheet is introduced and released this session.

You leave with: a first pass at a sample property in the model.

4

Deal screening and sourcing

Where listings and off-market opportunities come from, how brokers work, and how to screen a deal in minutes before spending hours on it.

You leave with: a pipeline tracker with your first screened deals.

5

Due diligence

Physical, financial, and legal diligence: what to inspect, what to request from a seller, and how to read a rent roll and a T-12.

You leave with: the diligence checklist and request list.

6

Financing

Agency, bank, and bridge loans; how lenders size debt; what changes when rates move. How to talk to a lender.

You leave with: a loan-sizing worksheet.

7

Tax rules for property owners

Depreciation, cost segregation, passive-activity rules, and the Real Estate Professional Status test as written in the tax code. Taught as education; students are directed to their own CPA for advice.

You leave with: a CPA conversation guide and time-log template.

8

Partnership and capital structures

How general and limited partners are organized, what a private placement is, and what securities rules require. Taught so students can recognize a well-structured deal.

You leave with: a glossary of partnership terms.

9

Asset management

What ownership looks like after closing: property management oversight, budgets, reporting, and the monthly review.

You leave with: a monthly asset-management review template.

10

Capstone: buyer interview and portfolio plan

Students present one underwritten deal to the instructors and receive structured feedback, then finish a written plan for their next twelve months of study and action.

You leave with: a completed capstone and certificate of completion.

Two group deal labs are scheduled between sessions, where a real listing is underwritten live on screen with the class. Deal labs are group instruction; no individual investment advice is given.

Questions

Before you enroll

Is this an investment? Does my tuition go into a property or fund?+
No. Tuition pays for instruction only. XSITE Academy does not pool student tuition into any property, fund, or security, and enrolling does not give you an ownership stake in anything. What you do with what you learn afterward is entirely your own decision.
Will I be asked to invest with XSITE Capital?+
No. XSITE Capital's deals are separate from this course. Some graduates later invest alongside XSITE Capital, and many don't; nothing in the curriculum requires it, and no session is a pitch for a specific deal.
What if I miss a live session?+
Every session is recorded and posted within 24 hours, so you can watch on your own schedule. Group deal labs are the exception: they run live twice per cohort, and joining live is how most students get feedback on their own screening work.
How does the refund work, exactly?+
Attend Sessions 1 and 2, live or recorded, and submit the Session 1 buy box assignment. Email support@xsiteacademy.com by 5:00 p.m. Eastern the Friday after Session 2, and we refund every dollar paid, including installments, to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.
Do you promise results?+
No. This is education, not a guarantee. We can't promise you'll close a deal, and we'd be skeptical of anyone who does. What we do promise is a structured curriculum, direct instructor access, and templates built from deals we've actually closed.
Is there anything else to buy after this?+
No. Tuition includes every tool, template, and the Legacy Multifamily Course listed in the delivery schedule above. There is no upsell inside the program and no subscription that continues after your installments are complete.
Who do I contact with a question?+
Email support@xsiteacademy.com and we'll respond within one business day.

Refund Policy+

Eligibility. Any student may request a full refund of tuition paid, including any installment already charged, provided all three conditions are met: (1) the student attended Sessions 1 and 2 live or watched the recordings; (2) the student submitted the written buy box assignment from Session 1; and (3) the request is emailed to support@xsiteacademy.com no later than 5:00 p.m. Eastern on the Friday following Session 2 of the student's cohort.

Process. We acknowledge refund requests within one business day and issue approved refunds to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Remaining installments are cancelled at the same time. Course access ends when the refund is issued.

After the refund window. Because proprietary templates and tools are released at Session 3, tuition is non-refundable after the window closes. Students who cannot complete a cohort for medical or family reasons may transfer their seat to the next cohort at no charge by emailing support.

Installment plans. Choosing the three-payment plan does not change the refund window. If a refund is approved, all installments already paid are returned and future installments are cancelled.
